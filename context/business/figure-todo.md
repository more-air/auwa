## SIX TESTS — ONE AT A TIME

*Change only one thing per test. Keep every test piece and label it.*

- [ ] **1. Layers.** Print the same figure at 0.2mm and at 0.08mm. Sand both. Compare.
- [ ] **2. Sanding time.** Sand one figure fully and time it with a clock. Write the number down.
      Order: 180 → 320 → 400 → 600. Foam block on the curves. Hardest spots: under the arms, between the legs, and the mark on the top of the head.
- [ ] **3. Stain.** Test stain on White Oak and on Black Walnut, several strengths. Aim: near-black, grain still visible.
- [ ] **4. Finish.** Sand three pieces the same. Leave one bare. Oil one with camellia oil. Wax and buff one. Choose by touch, not by photo.
- [ ] **5. The number.** Add the edition number to the model, underneath, between the legs. Print it. Check it is clean and readable.
- [ ] **6. Posting.** Pack one figure exactly as it will be sold. Post it abroad to a friend. Ask them to photograph the opening.
- [ ] **Weigh a finished figure.** If it feels too light, add steel shot through a hole underneath and glue a cap over it.

## MAKE THE FIRST FIVE

*Five finished pieces are enough to open the drop. Tom photographs them.*

- [ ] Design the obi band: logo, edition, number, on washi
- [ ] Write one card by hand as the template — your words, Japanese or both
- [ ] Make 3 complete pieces, box included, by early October
- [ ] Tom photographs, mid-October

**The two hours, per figure:**

1. Print with the number underneath — 10 min
2. Remove supports, clean up — 10 min
3. Sand 180 → 320 → 400 → 600 — 30-45 min
4. Stain (Sumi only), dry fully — 10 min
5. Wax and buff — 10 min
6. Paint the eyes — 8 min
7. Check the number underneath — 3 min
8. Fit to the base — 5 min
9. Sign the base, write the number — 2 min
10. Write the card by hand — 6 min
11. Pack: cloth, felt cradle, base flat underneath, card on top, lid, obi, outer carton — 14 min
12. Record which number went to whom — 4 min

**The figure lies down in the box. It never stands.**
