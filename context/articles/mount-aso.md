## THE SUBJECT

Mount Aso in Kumamoto, Kyushu: one of the largest active volcanic calderas in the world, with
villages, farms and roads inside the crater. Tom and Rieko were there **11–12 May 2018**,
GPS-confirmed at 32.89, 131.06. This is the same trip as the Kumano Kodo walk in late April, so the
two articles share a folder but nothing else. **Keep them separate.**

The Auwa angle is not "big volcano". It is people living inside one: grassland grazed for
centuries on the floor of a crater, a road winding through folded green hills, azaleas flowering on
ground that is still moving. Attention paid to a landscape that could end you.
