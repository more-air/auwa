## THE SUBJECT

Naoshima, the art island in the Seto Inland Sea: a former industrial island rebuilt around Tadao
Ando's concrete museums, the Art House Project in Honmura village, and Yayoi Kusama's yellow
pumpkin on the pier. Tom and Rieko visited in **January 2014**, in winter, which is the quiet
season and shows in the photographs — empty streets, low light, almost no other visitors.

**Read the fit honestly.** This is the weakest Auwa subject on the backlog: contemporary art rather
than craft or philosophy, and Auwa is not an art-tourism brand. It earns its place two ways, and
the piece should lean on both. First, **winter and emptiness** — everyone else photographs Naoshima
in summer crowds. Second, **the old village**, where the Art House Project put contemporary work
inside two-hundred-year-old houses. That join, new work inside old buildings, is close to what Auwa
argues about craft and continuity. Write that article, not a museum guide.

## ANGLE AND SEO

"Naoshima" is a competitive travel term (japan-guide, Benesse's own site, tour operators, design
magazines). Do not chase it flat. The winnable angle is **Naoshima in winter** and **the Art House
Project**, both of which are under-covered in English.

- **Primary keyword:** Naoshima in winter / Naoshima Art House Project
- **Secondary:** Honmura village, Tadao Ando Naoshima, Seto Inland Sea, Benesse Art Site
- **`seoTitle`** (max 45 chars): e.g. *Naoshima in Winter: The Empty Art Island*
- Internal links: `72-seasons` (season as the subject) and `yakushima-island` (island ↔ island).

**Note on age.** These photographs are from 2014 and Naoshima has changed since — new museums have
opened and the pumpkin was damaged and replaced in 2021. Say the visit was in winter 2014 rather
than implying it is current, and check nothing described has since closed.
